## Measured: default palettes, adjacent pairs, OKLab x 100

Minimum over normal, protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia for each neighbouring pair (series 1-2, 2-3, ...):

- TradingView `PALETTE` (light, dark, and `shadcn`'s fallbacks): 30.1, 14.0 (orange/teal, protan), 16.0 (teal/purple, deutan), 21.8, 19.1, 21.1, 24.9; wrap-around 8 -> 1 (pink/blue) passes too. **Lowest 14.0. No pair fails.**
- `accessible` (Okabe-Ito order): 29.2, 11.4 (orange/green, protan), 11.0 (green/vermillion, deutan), 10.7 (vermillion/pink, tritan), 9.6 (pink/sky blue, deutan), 15.4, 17.8. **Lowest 9.6. No pair fails.**
- The `other` token (pie and waffle "Other") is close to some palette hues: light `#A3A6AF` against cyan `#00BCD4` is 5.8 (protan) and against Okabe-Ito pink `#CC79A7` 4.3 (deutan); dark `#6B7080` against purple `#7B6BC6` is 4.2 (tritan) and against teal `#089981` 6.9 (deutan). "Other" is always last and is also named in the legend and tooltip, but a theme-wide check would have warned on default pies with an Other slice. The palettes were not changed.
- For reference, d3's category10 red and green (`#d62728`, `#2ca02c`) are 33.8 with normal vision and 3.9 under deuteranopia; its blue and green are 6.8 under tritanopia. Pure `#ff0000` and `#00ff00` stay 22.2 apart under deuteranopia because their lightness differs; the check measures what a reader sees, not hue names.

No gallery example warns under `light`, `dark`, `accessible`, `accessible-dark` or `shadcn` (tested in `tests/core/cvd.test.ts`).

## Conformance and ports

New conformance data: `waffleCells` (largest-remainder cases), validation cases for both charts, describe cases for both. The Go and Python ports do not know `waffle`, `beeswarm` or `waffleCells` yet, so `npm run test:ports` will fail on those cases until they are ported; the cross-field checks to port are waffle's `cells % 10` and `no_positive_values`, and beeswarm's `no_points` (path `x`). The colour check is a compile-time warning, not validation, so the ports need nothing for it.
