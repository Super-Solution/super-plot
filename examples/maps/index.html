Maps

Choropleth, bubbles, 3D columns, raised regions and flows, flat or on a globe. Super Plot ships no boundaries: the examples use invented islands, and you can import your own file below.

Drag to move. Shift + drag to turn and tilt. Ctrl/⌘ + scroll or pinch to zoom. Keyboard: arrows, Shift + arrows, + − 0.

Use your own boundaries

A GeoJSON FeatureCollection or a TopoJSON file, in longitude/latitude degrees. Pick a template if the file comes from a common source, so the join key and names are found for you. Which areas count as disputed, and how they are drawn, comes from your file; Super Plot draws every region the same unless you choose otherwise.
