Super Plot

Statistical charts for people and agents. Every chart on this page is one small JSON spec. Hover for tooltips, click a legend item to hide a series. This is one self-contained file: no server, no network.

Every chart type, light and dark

Bar, line, area, scatter, pie, donut, histogram, box plot, heatmap, radar, treemap and sankey, with a few variants. The default style follows TradingView's palette.

Restyle it without forking

The same three specs under four looks. Overrides are CSS variables, a theme object, or a colour in the spec itself.

Colour-vision safe preset

One word, theme: 'accessible': Okabe-Ito hues ordered to stay apart for deutan, protan and tritan vision; up and down are blue and vermillion, not green and red.

Many points
